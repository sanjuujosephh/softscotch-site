Softscotch: what we build

Softscotch is a digital marketing agency covering SEO, AI search visibility, Google and Meta ads, email and automation. Alongside client work, the team builds tools for marketers, founders and small businesses. Each product started as a problem the agency kept solving by hand for clients.

Our products

  • Softscotch: a full-service digital marketing agency that works as an outsourced marketing team for growing businesses.
  • PostInstantly: an AI post editor that learns your voice and turns ideas into posts for LinkedIn, X, Threads and Instagram.
  • BrandsForCreators: brands list a product, US creators who like it apply, and the brand picks who receives it in exchange for real content.
  • DarnItSEO: SEO, AEO and GEO audits with a ranked action plan, plus tracking of whether AI tools mention your site.
  • Marketing Newsfeed: the marketing updates that matter, from 30+ sources, with priority levels and an action list.
  • VerifiedWidget: a fast, lightweight widget that shows your real Google reviews on any website.
  • amlookingfor: a curated hub of directories, listing sites and launch platforms for founders and makers in the US.
