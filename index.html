Softscotch product reference

A feature by feature reference for the products built by Softscotch, the digital marketing agency.

PostInstantly

  • Ideas board with trending topics in your niche.
  • Three-question drafts that follow your writing style.
  • Editor with 30+ tools that scores hook, readability and length.
  • Calendar for LinkedIn, X, Threads and Instagram.
  • Repurposing of blogs, newsletters, videos, podcasts and PDFs.
  • Carousel, thread and infographic makers, plus voice to post.

Visit PostInstantly.

BrandsForCreators

  • Product listings with creator spots, deadlines and deliverables.
  • Applications with a 0 to 100 trust score.
  • Plain English creator search and an Instagram and TikTok tag scan.
  • Outreach sequences with a deliverability score that stop on reply.
  • Creator CRM, shared inbox and content library.

Visit BrandsForCreators.

DarnItSEO

  • 75+ checks turned into a ranked, plain English action plan.
  • AI prompt tracking across ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity and AI Overviews.
  • Competitor view, local rank grid, Google Business Profile audit and citation checks.
  • Search Console connection and content publishing to WordPress, Ghost, Shopify and Webflow.

Visit DarnItSEO.

Marketing Newsfeed

  • 30+ sources filtered down to what affects results.
  • Priority, summary, category and update type on every item.
  • Action list, platform status panel, glossary and critical email alerts.

Visit Marketing Newsfeed.

VerifiedWidget

  • Setup from a Google Maps link, with no API key.
  • Nine layouts, about 12 KB, no iframe.
  • JSON-LD schema for 14 business types and multi-location support.

Visit VerifiedWidget.

amlookingfor

  • People, Launches, Directories and Ranked sections for US founders and makers.

Visit amlookingfor.
